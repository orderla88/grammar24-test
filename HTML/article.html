Introduction

Article is a short word, that can determine noun's specificity, case, gender, quantity. For example:

  • In German language articles determine noun's gender.

    die Katze (cat, feminine)
    der Hund (dog, masculine)

  • In Spanish language articles determine noun's quantity.

    el perro (dog, single)
    las manos (hands, plural)

  • In English language articles determine noun's specificity. That is, whether noun is general or specific.
    I have an apple. (unspecific)
    The apple is green. (specific)

There are 2 types English articles:

  1. Indefinite articles
  2. Definite article

Indefinite Articles

Indefinite articles a/an come before single nouns, starting with consonant/vowel sounds1 accordingly.
Those nouns are:

  • general or unspecific;

    An apple a day,
    Keeps doctor away.

  • mentioned for the first time;

    I see a car outside.
    There is a girl inside it.

  • members of particular group;

    My dad is a doctor.
    Hannah is an actress

  • quantities or rates (meaning "one" or "per" );

    These nuts cost $5 a kilo.
    I work out twice a week

1 Initial sound, not letter:

an hour – ['aʊə], a hotel – [hoʊ'tel];

Definite Article

Definite articles the comes before:

  • specific single/plural nouns;

    The dog that bit me ran away.
    The books you wrote are great.

  • already mentioned (defined) nouns;

    I've bought a book recently.
    The book is about computers.

  • one of a kind nouns;

    The Sun is high in the sky.
    The Earth is hitting up.

  • newspapers;

    the Times,   the Chicago Tribune,
    the Guardian,   the Independent;

  • named groups of people;

    the Smiths, the Yankees,
    the Beatles, the Marines;

  • famous buildings;

    the Eiffel Tower, the White House,
    the Great Wall of China;

  • bodies of water, mountain ranges;

    the Mississippi,   the Atlantic,
    the Appalachian Mountains;

  • plural or multi-word country names;

    the United States of America,
    the Philipines,  the Netherlands;

  • adjectives in superlative degree;

    We've got the cheapest seats.
    This is the fastest car in the city.

Zero Article

We do not use any article before:

  • sports, subjects, languages;

    math,  football,  Chinese;

  • people and company names;

    John Smith,  Amy,  Apple;

  • city, district or street names;

    Wall Street,  Brooklyn,  Detroit;

  • days and months;

    Wednesday,  December;

  • meal names;

    breakfast,  lunch,  dinner;
