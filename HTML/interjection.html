Introduction

  • Interjection is word or phrase, that:

    • followed/preceded a brief pause;
    • expresses response to some event;
    • is syntactically independent from other words in a sentence;

    Interjection can be expressed by:

    • sound: Boo!
    • word: Hello.
    • expression: What the hell!?

  • Punctuation

    Interjection follows below rules in writing:

    • used alone – ends like a sentence;

      Hey!   Really?
      Good riddance.

    • starts the sentence – followed by a comma or ellipsis;

      Phew, that wasn't easy.  
      Oh... I am so sorry.

    • ends the sentence – preceded by comma or ellipsis;

      Nice evening, huh?
      I can't eat this... eww.

Primary Interjection

A sound uttered by a person, to express their response to some event.

Interjection example
Ahem Ahem, attention please!
Aargh Aargh, I hate this job!

Gosh Gosh, what a heat!
Ha-ha Ha-ha, I lost the game!
He-he He-he, that was funny.
Heh Heh, you can’t beat me.
Hmm Hmm, let me think about it.
Huh You didn’t expect that, huh?
Jeez Jeez, it's cold out here.
Meh Meh, not impressive.
Oh Oh, what a surprise!
Oops Oops, I did it again... .
Ouch Ouch, you're hurting me!
Phew Phew, that was close.
Psst Psst… can I talk to you?
Shh Shh, be quiet please.
Uh-oh Uh-oh, here they come.
Ugh Ugh, not that again!
Umm Umm, I guess you’re right.
Wow Wow, did you see that?
Yay Yay, we’re going to travel!
Yikes Yikes, I won't touch this.
Yoh Yoh, whassup my man?
Note the position of interjections.
Generally, we express our emotions first, then make a statement.

Onomatopoeia

A class of words, originated by mimicking some sounds.

bang boom beep
chirp clap crash
gulp klank mew
splash swoosh thump
pop pow vroom
wham woof zip

Onomatopoeia words are used as:

  • Interjections;

    Boom! And they disappeared... .

  • Nouns;

    We heard car's beep from the outside.

  • Verb;

    Birds were chirping happily.

Interjection vs Onomatopoeia

  • Interjection sound is uttered by a human;

    Uh-oh...  Oops!   Huh?  

  • Onomatopoeia sound is made by a human, animal or thing;

    Bang!  Mew...   Tik-tok;
