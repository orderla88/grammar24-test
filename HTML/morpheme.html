Introduction

Word consists of graphemes, phonemes and morphemes. We learned about graphemes and phonemes in the previous chapter.
Morpheme is the smallest meaningful part of word. It is categorized into:

  • Root – a primary part of word, to which other morphemes are attached.
  • Affix – a common term for prefix and suffix, which are attached to word’s root.

Root

Root (a.k.a. stem) is a primary part of word, to which other morphemes are attached. It can be native or borrowed.

  • Native roots in English words have Old Germanic origin. We often can guess their meaning and even use them alone as words.
    Prefix Root Suffix
    un- friend -ly
    - hard -ship
    re- action -
  • Borrowed roots are imported from languages like Greek, Latin and French, which belong to other language families. These roots often do not make sense on their own and need affixes to form a word.
    Prefix Latin root Suffix
    - dict (speak) -ate
    de- struct (build) -
    in- carcer (jail) -ate
    Prefix Greek root Suffix
    sym- path (feel) -y
    syn- chron (time) -ize
    de- hydr (water) -ate

Prefix with Hyphen

Add hyphen after prefix in following cases:

  • after prefixes all-, ex -, self- prepended to a noun or adjective;

    all-around, ex-wife, self-taught

  • after any prefix prepended to a proper noun or adjective;

    non-English, anti-Trump, ex-Nigerian

Suffix

Suffix is a part of word, that is attached to the end of root. Suffixes are divided into two types:

  1. Derivational suffixes form words themselves, like nouns, adjectives, verbs and adverbs;
  2. Inflectional suffixes form words' grammatical properties, like conjugation, plural form, comparison degrees;

Inflectional Suffixes

  1. Suffixes -s/es, -ing, -d/ed are used in verb conjugation.

    Example Sufix
    opens -s/es
    opening -ing
    opened -d/ed

  2. Suffixes -er/-r, -est/-st are used in adjective/adverb comparison degrees

    Example Sufix
    taller, later -er
    tallest, latest -est

  3. Suffixes -s/-es are used in noun plural form.

    Example Sufix
    blankets, trees -s
    buses, dishes -es
