Introduction

Sentence is one or more words, expressing a complete thought. It starts with capital letter and ends with a punctuation mark. The latter can be a period, question mark or exclamation mark.1

Do we go to the park?
Yes, we go to the park.
Hurray!

Depending on whether sentence expresses statement, reaction, command or question, it is categorized into following four types.
