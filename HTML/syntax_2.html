Clause

A clause of sentence is a pair of subject + predicate. It is the main structure of sentence, on which other parts (like adverbial or complement) depend.1
There are 2 types of clause:

  • Active clause – it has an active predicate;

    Police arrested a man.
    They are interrogating him.

  • Passive clause – it has a passive predicate;

    The man was arrested.
    He is being interrogated.
