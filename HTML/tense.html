Introduction

English language has 3 tenses:

  • Past Tense
  • Present Tense
  • Future Tense

Each tense has 4 states:

  • Simple – repeated action or state, fact.
  • Progressive1 – ongoing action or state.
  • Perfect – finished action or state.
  • Perfect Continuous – period of action or state.
1 "Progressive" in US = "Continuous" in UK.

Verb Forms

English tenses are made by verbs.
Therefore, we will need verb forms from chapter §8 Verb.

Form Formula Reg.V Irreg.V
Base form verb call eat
Infinitive to + verb to call to eat
Present Participle verb+ing calling eating
Past Participle verb+ed called eaten
Past Tense verb+ed called ate
Conjugation* verb+(e)s calls eats

* All verbs are conjugated only in the 3rd single Person by adding -s/-es. The only exception is verb be, which changes its form in every Person.

Present Tense

  • Simple

    • Definition: general action/state at present;
    • Formula: base form;
    • Conjugation: only in the 3rd single Person by adding suffix -s/-es;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I verb. We verb.
    2nd You verb. You verb.
    3rd He/She/It verb+s. They verb.
    Examples
    Sen. 2nd single/plural 3rd single
    + You read. He reads.
    - You do not read. He does not read.
    ? Do you read? Does he read?
    Auxiliary verb 'do' is also conjugated according to Person.

  • Continuous

    • Definition: currently happening action/state;
    • Formula: be + Present Participle;
    • Conjugation: only be is conjugated in every Person;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I am verb+ing. We are verb+ing.
    2nd You are verb+ing. You are verb+ing.
    3rd He/She/It is verb+ing. They are verb+ing.
    Examples
    Sen. 2nd single/plural 3rd single
    + You are reading. He is reading.
    - You are not reading. He is not reading.
    ? Are you reading? Is he reading?

  • Perfect

    • Definition: recently finished action/state;
    • Formula: have + Past Participle;
    • Conjugation: have turns to has in the 3rd single Person;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I have verb+ed. We have verb+ed.
    2nd You have verb+ed. You have verb+ed.
    3rd He/She/It has verb+ed. They have verb+ed.
    Examples
    Sen. Regular Verb Irregular Verb 2
    + She has finished. She has come.
    - She has not finished. She has not come.
    ? Has she finished? Has she come?
    2 Irregular verbs do not form Past Participle form by formula 'verb+ed'.

  • Perfect Continuous

    • Definition: period of action/state up untill now;
    • Formula: have been + Present Participle;
    • Conjugation: have turns to has in the 3rd Single Person;
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I have been verb+ing. We have been verb+ing.
    2nd You have been verb+ing. You have been verb+ing.
    3rd He/She/It has been verb+ing. They have been verb+ing.
    Example
    Sen. 3rd single
    + He has been working for 2 hours.
    - He has not been working for 2 hours.
    ? Has he been working for 2 hours?

Past Tense

  • Simple

    • Definition: general action/state in past;
    • Formula: Past Tense;
    • Conjugation: none;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I verb+ed. We verb+ed.
    2nd You verb+ed. You verb+ed.
    3rd He/She/It verb+ed. They verb+ed.
    Example:
    Sen. Regular Irregular 3
    + They worked. They came.
    - They did not work. They did not come.
    ? Did they work? Did they come?
    3 Irregular verbs do not form Past Tense form by formula 'verb+ed'.

  • Continuous

    • Definition: ongoing action/state at past point;
    • Formula: was + Present Participle;
    • Conjugation: was turns into were in plural Person;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I was verb+ing. We were verb+ing.
    2nd You was verb+ing. You were verb+ing.
    3rd He/She/It was verb+ing. They were verb+ing.
    Example
    Sen. 3rd plural
    + Kids were playing outside, when I came.
    - Kids were not playing outside, when I came.
    ? Were kids playing outside, when I came?

  • Perfect

    • Definition: action/state finished by past point;
    • Formula: had + Past Participle;
    • Conjugation: no;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I had verb+ed. We had verb+ed.
    2nd You had verb+ed. You had verb+ed.
    3rd He/She/It had verb+ed. They had verb+ed.
    Example
    Sen. 3rd single
    + They had finished the work by 5 PM.
    - They had not finished the work by 5 PM.
    ? Had they finished the work by 5 PM?

  • Perfect Continuous

    • Definition: period of action/state in past;
    • Formula: had been + Present Participle;
    • Conjugation: no;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I had been verb+ing. We had been verb+ing.
    2nd You had been verb+ing. You had been verb+ing.
    3rd He/She/It had been verb+ing. They had been verb+ing.
    Example
    Sen. 3rd single
    + He had been working abroad for 2 years.
    - He had not been working abroad for 2 years.
    ? Had he been working abroad for 2 years?

Future Tense

  • Simple

    • Definition: general action/state in future;
    • Formula: will + Base Form;
    • Conjugation: no;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I will verb. We will verb.
    2nd You will verb. You will verb.
    3rd He/She/It will verb. They will verb.
    Example
    Sen. 3rd single
    + The ship will arrive tomorrow.
    - The ship will not arrive tomorrow.
    ? Will the ship arrive tomorrow?

  • Progressive

    • Definition: ongoing action/state at future point;
    • Formula: will be + Present Participle;
    • Conjugation: no;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I will be verb+ing. We will be verb+ing.
    2nd You will be verb+ing. You will be verb+ing.
    3rd He/She/It will be verb+ing. They will be verb+ing.
    Example
    Sen. 3rd single
    + They will be leaving at 5pm.
    - They will not be leaving at 5pm.
    ? Will they be leaving at 5pm?

  • Perfect

    • Definition: action/state finished by future point;
    • Formula: will have + Past Participle;
    • Conjugation: no;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I will have verb+ed. We will have verb+ed.
    2nd You will have verb+ed. You will have verb+ed.
    3rd He/She/It will have verb+ed. They will have verb+ed.
    Example
    Sen. 3rd single
    + Train will have departed by 10am.
    - Train will not have departed 10am.
    ? Will train have departed by 10am.

  • Perfect Progressive

    • Definition: period of action/state by future point;
    • Formula: will have been + Present Participle;
    • Conjugation: no;
    Conjugation
    Per. Single Plural
    1st I will have verb+ing. We will have been verb+ing.
    2nd You will have been verb+ing. You will have been verb+ing.
    3rd He/She/It will have been verb+ing. They will have been verb+ing.
    Example
    Sen. 1st single
    + By 2028, I will have been living here for 10 years.
    - By 2028, I will not have been living here for 10 years.
    ? By 2028, will I have been living here for 10 years.

Summary

Let's sum up English tenses by conjugating regular/irregular verbs.

  • Regular verb "live" makes Past Participle and Past Tense forms by formula verb+(e)d:
    Tense Simple Continuous Perfect P.Continuous
    Past lived was/were living had lived had been living
    Present lives am/is/are living have/has lived have/has been living
    Future will live will be living will have lived will have been living
  • Irregular verb "come" does not make Past Participle and Past Tense forms by that formula:
    Tense Simple Continuous Perfect P.Continuous
    Past came was/were coming had come had been coming
    Present come(s) am/is/are coming have/has come have/has been coming
    Future will come will be coming will have come will have been coming
